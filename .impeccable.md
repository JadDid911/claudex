## Design Context

### Users

Developers and technical creators working for long stretches in PowerShell, Windows Terminal, or wmux. They want one durable conversation that delegates work across Codex and Claude subscriptions without hiding who is active, losing context, or weakening workspace safety.

### Brand Personality

Calm, kinetic, opinionated. The room should feel as deliberate as the official Codex and Claude CLIs, with a little personality in its activity language, but never become chatty, theatrical, or dashboard-like.

### Aesthetic Direction

A refined terminal-native control room: compact information hierarchy, strong actor identity, progressive command disclosure, keyboard-first interaction, restrained color, and one ephemeral activity surface. It should preserve ordinary scrollback and work cleanly in both interactive TTYs and deterministic redirected output.

### Design Principles

1. Show decisions and outcomes; collapse lifecycle plumbing.
2. Keep the prompt visually dominant and advanced controls one slash command away.
3. Make provider, model, effort, delegation mode, and writer ownership legible at a glance.
4. Prefer concise recovery guidance over raw upstream diagnostics.
5. Preserve terminal conventions, accessibility, and plain-text fallback behavior.
6. Earn every persistent line: ephemeral work moves; durable results stay.

### Quality Bar

Flagship public GitHub release: complete interaction states, polished copy, deterministic tests, no new runtime dependencies, and clean behavior in PowerShell, Windows Terminal, wmux, and non-TTY automation.
